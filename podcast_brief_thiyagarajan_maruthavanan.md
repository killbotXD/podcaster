# Podcast Brief: Thiyagarajan Maruthavanan

## Guest Summary

Thiyagarajan 'Rajan' Maruthavanan is the Managing Partner and Co-founder of Upekkha, a SaaS accelerator that has worked with over 100 early-stage startups. He's also a Forbes Business Council member, angel investor, and thought leader who writes extensively about AI's impact on SaaS, having previously co-founded Motvik and been involved with ProductNation/iSPIRT.

## Research Summary

Thiyagarajan Maruthavanan is a seasoned SaaS expert and startup ecosystem builder who has transitioned from advisor to founder to accelerator leader. As Managing Partner and Co-founder of Upekkha, he has worked with over 100 early-stage startups and recently took on the CEO role as the accelerator pivots toward AI SaaS and deeptech. His background includes co-founding Motvik, involvement with ProductNation/iSPIRT, and serving as a Forbes Business Council member. He's also an active angel investor backing 150+ global Indian founders. Rajan is a prolific writer and thought leader, regularly publishing insights on LinkedIn and Medium about the intersection of AI and SaaS, including provocative pieces questioning whether traditional SaaS models are becoming obsolete. His recent writings explore themes like 'Agents as Capital' and provide guidance for founders navigating the AI transition, positioning him as someone who understands both the technical and business implications of this shift.
