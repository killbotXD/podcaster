# Podcast Brief: Jeff Green

## Guest Summary

Jeff Green is the co-founder, CEO and Chairman of The Trade Desk, the world's largest independent demand-side platform for programmatic advertising. Previously, he co-founded AdECN, which was acquired by Microsoft in 2007, before founding The Trade Desk in 2009 with Dave Pickles.

## Research Summary

The research reveals Jeff Green as a serial entrepreneur and visionary in the advertising technology space. After co-founding AdECN and selling it to Microsoft in 2007, he spent two years at the tech giant before co-founding The Trade Desk in 2009 with Dave Pickles. The company, which specializes in programmatic advertising and data-driven ad buying, went public in 2016 and has grown to become the world's largest independent demand-side platform. Green has been an active public figure, appearing on major media outlets including 60 Minutes, Bloomberg, and CNBC to discuss industry issues ranging from fake news to consumer privacy. His leadership style appears to emphasize both business success and social responsibility, positioning him as a thoughtful leader in an often-criticized industry.
