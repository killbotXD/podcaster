# Podcast Brief: Andrej Karpathy

## Guest Summary

Andrej Karpathy is a leading AI researcher who was a founding member of OpenAI, served as Director of AI at Tesla, and is now founder of Eureka Labs, an AI-native education platform. He's known for making complex AI concepts accessible through his educational content and for coining terms like 'vibe coding' and 'agentic engineering' that capture emerging trends in AI-assisted programming.

## Research Summary

Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher and educator who has been at the forefront of the field's major developments. After co-founding OpenAI and serving as Director of AI at Tesla's Autopilot team, he recently launched Eureka Labs, an 'AI-native' education platform. He's particularly known for his exceptional ability to explain complex AI concepts through educational content like his 'Neural Networks: Zero to Hero' series, earning recognition for what's called 'The Karpathy Effect.' Recently, he's been vocal about the current state of AI development, arguing that AGI is still a decade away while coining terms like 'vibe coding' and 'agentic engineering' to describe how AI is changing software development. His perspective combines deep technical expertise with practical insights about AI's limitations and a vision for how education must evolve in the age of AI.
