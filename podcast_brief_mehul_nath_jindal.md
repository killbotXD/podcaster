# Podcast Brief: Mehul Nath Jindal

## Guest Summary

Mehul Nath Jindal is the CEO and Co-Founder of BharatX, a Y Combinator-backed fintech startup focused on democratizing credit for India's middle class through embedded consumer credit solutions. A NIT Trichy graduate who started stock trading at 15 and built a profitable e-commerce business at 17, he's been recognized in Forbes India's 30 Under 30 and actively shares insights about Indian fintech through his Substack publication.

## Research Summary

Mehul Nath Jindal is a remarkably young entrepreneur who has demonstrated business acumen from an early age. Starting as a stock trader at 15 and building a successful e-commerce business as a teenager, he co-founded BharatX while studying at NIT Trichy with three classmates. The company focuses on embedded consumer credit solutions for India's underserved middle class and has gained recognition through Y Combinator backing and inclusion in Forbes India's 30 Under 30 list. Beyond building his company, Mehul is also a thought leader in the Indian fintech space, writing extensively on his Substack publication 'FinTech India!' where he shares unfiltered perspectives on the industry and VC ecosystem. His unique combination of early entrepreneurial success, academic excellence, and industry thought leadership makes him an compelling voice on the future of financial inclusion in India.
